FAQs

When does AccraWireless launch?

We’re rolling out in phases across Greater Accra, starting with the areas that have the most waitlist signups. Everyone on the list hears on WhatsApp as soon as their area is ready.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining is free and doesn’t commit you to anything. You decide whether to sign up once we reach your area.

How much will it cost?

Prices are announced before launch. Waitlist members see them first and get early-member rates.

Is the data really unlimited?

Yes. Every plan is unmetered, with no monthly cap and no slow-down after heavy use.

Which areas will you cover?

All of Greater Accra over time, from Kasoa to Tema and north to Amasaman and Oyarifa. The order depends on where people sign up.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name, number and area only to plan coverage and tell you when we’re ready. We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
